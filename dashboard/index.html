COMPASS · K-NEAREST-NEIGHBOUR COMPS

Every pre-token protocol, priced by its closest trading twins.

Compass finds the eight tokenized protocols in the snapshot whose shape — TVL, chain breadth, age — most resembles each protocol that records no token, and prices it at the twins' median market-cap / TVL multiple. No hardcoded names: the same distance metric prices every row.

How the compass points

The distance

Features per protocol: log10(TVL), log10(chain count), log10(age days + 30), each standardised over the 144 tokenized peers. Distance is weighted Euclidean (weights 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.5) plus a category term: 0 for an exact category match, 0.6 when either side records no category, 1.25 for a different category. Ties break by slug.

The price

Implied multiple = median of the 8 nearest twins' mcap/TVL. Implied value = TVL × multiple × momentum (7-day TVL change, weight 0.35, clamped) × survival (age with no token, 0.85–1.15) × breadth (extra chains, up to +20%). Every factor is shown on the row's detail, line by line.

The fallback — stated, not hidden

If fewer than 4 twins sit within distance 2.5, the row is priced by its category's median peer multiple, shrunk toward the global median, and its detail says so. A protocol here "records no token" in this snapshot — that is what the data says, never proof it has none.
